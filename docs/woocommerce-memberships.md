# WooCommerce Memberships

> Adapter: `includes/adapters/woocommerce-memberships.php`. Suite: `tests/woocommerce-memberships.test.js`. Family: `memberships`. Audited against 1.30.0.

WooCommerce Memberships (SkyVerge) stores each membership as a `wc_user_membership` post: `post_author` is the member, `post_parent` the plan, `post_status` one of the plugin's own `wcm-active`, `wcm-delayed`, `wcm-complimentary`, `wcm-pending` (pending cancellation), `wcm-paused`, `wcm-expired`, `wcm-cancelled`. Dates (`_start_date`, `_end_date`, `_paused_date`, `_cancelled_date`) are MySQL datetimes in UTC; the end date slides forward while a membership is paused, and the plugin's own getter (`get_end_date()`) applies that shift. Plans are `wc_membership_plan` posts with `_access_method`, `_access_length` and `_product_ids` meta; their rules live in the option `wc_memberships_rules`. Notes are comments of type `user_membership_note` that the plugin hides from every normal comment query, so they are only reachable through `get_notes()`.

The plugin ships `wc/v3/memberships/members` and `/plans`, but the members list cannot search below the v4 namespace, has no ordering, and answers neither notes nor counts; the plans routes are read-only and carry no member counts. Minn therefore:

- lists memberships through `WP_Query` on the CPT, exactly the way the plugin's own list screen and REST controller do (status, plan, customer and product filters map onto `post_status`, `post_parent`, `author` and the `_product_id` meta; the date window and the status chart both work on `post_date`), and resolves each row through `wc_memberships_get_user_membership()`
- searches members as accounts (login, email, display name, then first and last name) and lists their memberships, since a member is a WordPress user
- pauses, resumes and cancels through `pause_membership()`, `activate_membership()` and `cancel_membership()`, which are status transitions: the note, the paused interval, the expiry schedule, the member role and any email all happen in the plugin's `transition_post_status` handler, never in Minn
- sets an end date the way the plugin's own edit screen does: a site-local day converted to UTC before `set_end_date()`, a date in the past expires the membership unless it is cancelled, a future date on an expired membership reactivates it, and a blank date clears the end
- adds notes through `add_note( $text, $notify )`, so "email this note to the member" sends the plugin's Membership Note email
- transfers through `transfer_ownership()` and surfaces the plugin's own refusal messages (same customer, already a member of that plan)
- grants a membership by hand through `wc_memberships_create_user_membership()` after the one-membership-per-user-and-plan check their REST controller does (their creator does not), then leaves a note naming who granted it
- deletes through `deleteUserMembership()`, whose hook unschedules the expiry, fixes the member role and drops orphan profile-field values; the plugin removes Trash for this type, so delete is permanent and the confirm says so

Never call `is_active()` or `is_delayed()` from a read path: both write on read (they can expire, delay or activate the membership as a side effect). Rows use `has_status()`.

Capabilities are the plugin's own: `manage_woocommerce_user_memberships` gates the members list, routes and bulk delete (their CSV export and bulk delete check exactly this); `manage_woocommerce_membership_plans` gates the Plans view; every write also asks the per-object `edit_post` / `delete_post`, which `map_meta_cap` resolves to the `*_user_membership` primitives. Both custom caps are granted to shop managers and administrators on every `init` by the plugin. Editors and authors get nothing.

## The membership page

A row opens `/memberships/{id}` (the collection's `open` primitive), a record page in the same two-column shape as a product: Membership (plan and status comboboxes, Member since and Expires date pickers, a Remove end date link, paused/cancelled/time-active facts), Billing (the order, product and, when WooCommerce Subscriptions is active, the subscription behind it, or a note that it was granted by hand) and Notes (composer with an "email this note to the member" switch, newest first, delete per note) in the main column; Member (avatar, email, username, member since, billing address, View customer, an inline Transfer form), Plans (every membership this member holds, with a link to each, plus Add a plan for the plans they do not hold yet) and Profile fields (read-only) in the sidebar. The header carries the status pill, Open in WooCommerce and a ⋯ menu with Pause / Resume / Cancel / Delete.

It reads one route, `GET minn-admin/v1/wcm/members/{id}`, which returns the whole page model (member, plan, plan and status vocabularies, site-local dates, order/product/subscription, profile answers, notes, the member's other memberships, per-object `can` flags). Save is `POST` to the same route with `plan_id`, `status`, `start_date` and `end_date`: only fields that arrived and changed are written, the plan moves through `wp_update_post( post_parent )` exactly like their REST controller (refusing a plan the member already holds), the status through `update_status()`, the start through `set_start_date()`, and the end through the same past-expires / future-revives / blank-clears rule as the row action. The save bar appears only when the form differs from the model that arrived, and Discard rebuilds from that copy. Note deletion is `DELETE …/notes/{note}`, which checks the comment is a `user_membership_note` on that membership before `wp_delete_comment()`, the plugin's own delete-note handler. Quick view stays the modal from the list.

## The plan page

A Plans row opens `/membership-plans/{id}`, and Add plan on the Plans view creates one by name (open it to fill the rest) while `/membership-plans/new` is the full form. The page is the product-page shape: General (name, slug, description, grant access upon manual / registration / purchase with a product picker, membership length unlimited / specific / fixed dates), one card per rule type (Restrict content, Restrict products, Purchasing discounts) as a list of rules rather than a canvas, Members area section switches, a Status card and a Members card with counts by status. Each rule row is a content-type combobox (post types and taxonomies from the plugin's own `WC_Memberships_Admin_Membership_Plan_Rules` lists, which is the class its meta box uses and which loads under REST by design), an object picker over `GET minn-admin/v1/wcm/lookup?target=&q=` (posts of that type or terms of that taxonomy; empty means all of them), an access schedule (immediately or after N days/weeks/months/years), only-members-can view or purchase for products, and percentage or fixed amount plus an active switch for discounts.

`GET /wcm/plans/{id}` (or `/wcm/plans/blank`) returns the page model with every vocabulary; `POST /wcm/plans` creates through the plugin's `createPlan()` action and `POST /wcm/plans/{id}` updates. Both then apply the General fields the way the plugin's meta box does (access method, reset the length meta and re-set it, products only under purchase, members-area sections through `set_members_area_sections()`), and the rules through a thin subclass of the plugin's `SetPlanRules` action that exposes its protected `configureRule()`: every rule the page sent back with its id keeps that id (the rules store updates in place on a matching id), rules the page dropped are deleted through `delete_rules()`, and validation of targets, schedules and discounts is the plugin's own plus a target check against its lists. Duplicate (`POST /wcm/plans/{id}/duplicate`) creates a draft copy through the same path with fresh rule ids; the plugin's admin-only duplicate handler is not loaded under REST. Delete runs `deletePlan()`, which refuses while active members exist (their `user_has_cap` filter also withholds `delete_post` in that state, so the route reads the refusal back as the real reason) and otherwise cascades every membership and rule on the plan, which the confirm says.

Email content stays in WooCommerce (it exists only once the plugin's emails are enabled in WooCommerce settings), as do grant-access-retroactively, CSV import/export, profile-field definitions and the members directory; the status card links to the import/export page.
